ExpiryNest Support

ExpiryNest keeps your pantry records on your device. Add food from Pantry, enter the package date, and act on the Use Soon queue.

Reminders

Allow notifications and check that device battery settings permit local alerts. ExpiryNest does not send remote push notifications.

Backup

Open History to export CSV inventory/history or a complete JSON backup.

Food safety

Dates are memory aids. Follow package storage advice and discard food that appears unsafe.
